Shared services do not require permanent residents, local production, open access to every country, or profitability.

Evaluate on **2036-12-31** using the complete root criteria; inadequate evidence leaves the outcome unresolved, not failed through lack of reporting. Inherit the whole path, but earlier deadline milestones need not persist throughout later centuries.

## Probability and rationale

**Conditional probability: 65%; confidence: low.** Dated procurement and multiple infrastructure ambitions make at least one functioning shared system more plausible than none over ten years. The 65/35 split nevertheless reserves substantial weight for schedule, integration, access, and funding failures; no base-rate model establishes 65%.

This is an uncalibrated subjective starting estimate from the selective evidence in [forecasts/index](./index.md) and the repository's reasoning, conditional on the full ancestral path, not just the immediate parent.
