# No lunar independence after the missed household milestone in 3026

Root: [[forecasts/index]] · Parent: [[forecasts/local-supply-no-households-2126]]

This leaf is not yet expanded: later expansion is not assigned zero probability, and non-lunar pathways remain possible.

## Scenario and resolution

After early S and L but no H in 2126, **I is not met** at 3026. This includes later households that remain dependent, ongoing robotic or rotating operations, and lunar abandonment. It does not require households to remain absent throughout the intervening centuries.

Evaluate on **3026-12-31** using the complete root criteria; inadequate evidence leaves the outcome unresolved, not failed through lack of reporting. Inherit the whole path, but earlier deadline milestones need not persist throughout later centuries.

## Probability and rationale

**Conditional probability: 80%; confidence: very low.** The 80% complement combines the possibility that residence never meets the test with failure to achieve industrial and biological independence after residence develops. It is not an 80% estimate of disaster, stagnation, or the absence of all space civilization. Each causal mechanism would need its own future fork.

This is an uncalibrated subjective starting estimate from the selective evidence in [[forecasts/index]] and the repository's reasoning, conditional on the full ancestral path, not just the immediate parent.

## Validation and revision signals

Track household retention, essential import inventories, actual component replacement and food production, and human lifecycle health. Resolve only from adequate evidence of the I criterion failing, not from an assumption that unobserved societies do not exist.
