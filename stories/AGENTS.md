# Story rules

Applies to stories in both `stories/` and `stories-tw/`. Before writing or changing a story, read [worldbuilding and validation rules](../canon/AGENTS.md), [the recording workflow](../sessions/AGENTS.md), and [format and language rules](../templates/AGENTS.md).

- Use [the story template](../templates/story.md) and its [Taiwan Traditional Chinese counterpart](../templates/story-tw.md). Store stories at `stories/<slug>.md` with matching paths under `stories-tw/`.
- State the story's branch and assumptions. Stories may use different branches or parallel timelines under the canon guide's scope and consistency rules.
- Link every technology, place, and institution the story uses to its wiki entry. A new one spawns a draft wiki entry; read [wiki rules](../wiki/AGENTS.md) and validate it before treating its feasibility as established.
- Story events remain within their declared scope. Apply the canon guide's approval rules before promoting invented setting details or adding dated events to the canon timeline.
