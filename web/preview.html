Observable Milestone Criteria

Code Milestone Verification Standard
S Shared Services At least one lunar surface system providing 2+ service types to 2+ independently funded customer programs for 3 consecutive calendar years.
L Local Consumables At least 10 t/yr of lunar-derived water or oxygen used by a lunar customer (≥20% of its total consumption of that product) for 3 consecutive years.
H Households At least 100 continuous residents at one lunar site for 10 years, including ≥10 households treating it as primary residence rather than shift rotation.
I Verified Independence 30 consecutive years without Earth resupply or essential Earth-side operations, including local maintenance cycles and locally born cohorts reaching adulthood.

Scientific Validation

Physics · Energy, Power & Momentum Recoil

Using E = ½mv² and P_avg = E / t, accelerating a 100 t spacecraft to 10 km/s requires 5 TJ useful kinetic energy (5.56 TJ electrical at 90% efficiency). Launch pulses reach gigawatt scale, requiring energy storage between departures, and every launch imparts equal-and-opposite recoil to the accelerator station that must be recovered with reaction mass.

Consistency · Canon & Branch Scope

Verified against canon/laws.md and canon/timeline.md with no canon conflicts. Remains an optional engineering branch; other futures do not inherit this infrastructure automatically.

Motive & Adoption Conditions

Economic advantage requires high recurring traffic to amortize heavy capital hardware. Physical feasibility alone does not prove competitiveness against onboard propulsion or orbital tugs.

Laws of Physics

status: canon scope: shared across all branches
Law 01 · Relativity & Causality

No mass, energy, or information travels faster than light c in vacuum. All interplanetary and interstellar coordination obeys light-time latency.

Law 02 · Conservation & Thermodynamics

Every velocity change obeys conservation of momentum, and every power or computing system in vacuum must reject waste heat radiatively.
