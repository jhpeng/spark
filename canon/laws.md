# Laws

Known physics is binding. These are the laws that most often constrain civilization-scale ideas; any established physics not listed here is binding too.

## Relativity

- **No faster-than-light travel or signalling.** Nothing carrying mass, energy, or information exceeds *c*. No warp drives, wormhole travel, hyperspace, or ansible.
  - Consequence: interstellar civilization is light-lagged. A colony 10 ly away is at least 20 years from a reply.
- **Time dilation is real.** Fast travellers age less than those who stay home (γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²)). Relativistic trips cost exactly this in social and historical terms.
- **Mass-energy equivalence.** E = mc² caps every energy source; total conversion (antimatter) is the ceiling.

## Conservation and thermodynamics

- **Momentum is conserved.** Every drive throws reaction mass or pushes on something external (light, a magnetic field, a beam). No reactionless drives.
- **Rocket equation applies.** Δv = vₑ ln(m₀/m₁). Mass ratios grow exponentially with Δv.
- **Energy is conserved, entropy increases.** Every machine, habitat, and computer must reject waste heat; at scale, radiator area is a real design limit.

## Cosmology

- **The expanding universe limits reach.** Galaxies beyond the cosmic event horizon (~16 billion ly) can never be reached, even at *c*. The reachable universe shrinks over time.
- **Local Group is the practical domain.** Beyond it, dark-energy expansion isolates galaxy groups permanently.

## Biology (for baseline humans)

- **Radiation, microgravity, and timescales limit unmodified human bodies.** Long journeys need shielding, artificial gravity, biological modification, or non-biological minds. Which of these humanity uses is a prediction, not a law.

## Speculative physics

Physics that is allowed on paper but unconfirmed (exotic matter, Alcubierre metrics, new particles) is **not** available. If future discovery is part of the story, it gets its own `speculative` entry and can never be load-bearing for canon.
