# Session: Initial forecast tree from existing repository scenarios

## Ideas discussed

- The user requested a sub-agent to construct predictions from the repository's existing work after the forecast rules were established. A delegated author developed the paired forecast files; the parent agent integrated navigation, unresolved checks, and validation.
- The first tree follows lunar-led expansion: shared services, local material supply, household residence, and demonstrated independence from Earth support. A delayed-services path preserves the possibility of later development after a missed near-term milestone. Each fork distinguishes a capability from its complement rather than treating motives that can coexist as competing outcomes.
- Near-term indicators and dated outcomes connect the five-to-ten-year view to fifty-, hundred-, and thousand-year horizons. Unexpanded leaves retain unspecified later possibilities; the tree does not exhaust orbital, Martian, or other routes to expansion.
- Probabilities are initial subjective estimates conditional on the full ancestral scenario. They are not measurements, calibrated model outputs, or user-approved canon. Confidence is recorded separately; long-horizon estimates remain especially uncertain.
- Physics validation is conditional: the existing laws and engineering constraints remain binding, and unresolved manufacturing, life-support, transport, and cost questions stay open. A public program does not demonstrate a working mine; local production does not demonstrate a household community; residence does not demonstrate independent survival.
- Review distinguished outcome criteria from possible motives: demonstrated independence can qualify regardless of whether its sponsors deliberately pursued civilization backup. Earlier milestone windows do not require uninterrupted persistence between deadlines, and revised criteria cannot replace the scoring target of an older forecast.
- Consistency and motives: public procurement can supply early demand, but later users need funded purposes and accessible services. The tree preserves limited development and continuing import dependence. It introduces no approved future date or retcon.

## Decisions

- User authorization covers creating the forecast content with a sub-agent. It does not promote the existing wiki scenarios or new probabilities to canon.
- Publish synchronized English and Taiwan Traditional Chinese trees using the existing forecast rules, with observable criteria, evidence dates, confidence, reasons for estimates, and session provenance.
- Scope the first tree to lunar-led pathways. Optional AI demand, development limits, exports, orbital computing, and modular accelerators retain their separate premises and unresolved checks.
- Future sessions may revise these forecasts without human approval while preserving prior estimates and the reasons for material changes.
- Validation passed for both language trees: matching metadata, connected acyclic parent/child structure, reciprocal navigation, sibling deadlines and probability totals, total probability across the current leaf frontier, and local links. No canon or wiki claims were changed.
