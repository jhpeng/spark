# Session on lunar liquid oxygen buyers

## Ideas discussed

- The user asked to continue the reasoning in Traditional Chinese. The choice to follow the delayed-services path, its delivery thresholds and both new probability estimates are agent extrapolations.
- A cargo program could finance oxygen production by committing to repeated purchases. If that program reserves the available loading slots, the contract that helps build the plant could also limit sales to a second fleet.
- A 2020 study modeled 10 tonnes of oxygen production in 225 days. A 2025 ground test demonstrated oxygen liquefaction in a prototype tank. Neither supplies lunar customer receipts. At NASA's reference metabolic rate, six crew would consume about 1.96 tonnes of breathing oxygen per year. Two assumed 5-tonne spacecraft deliveries reach the forecast's annual threshold. [System study](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20205008303) · [Tank tests](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20250000432) · [Life support baseline](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210024855)

## Decisions

- Extend the path that missed shared services in 2051 and met the milestone in 2076. Score regular delivery in 2099–2101 and buyer diversity at a qualifying site in 2124–2126. Confidence in both forks is very low. All sources predate the tree's 2026-11-24 evidence cutoff.
- The branch is consistent with the 2026 population baseline and uses conventional extraction, liquefaction and mass accounting. Mine performance, contracted flights and funding remain conditional. [Physical laws](../canon/laws.md) · [Timeline](../canon/timeline.md)
